SparkEntry
Enter expenses in a flash.
SparkEntry is a single-file web app for quick expense and mileage capture in the field. Add an entry, attach a photo of the receipt, and it totals your mileage from start and stop odometer readings. Entries live in your phone's browser — no account, no sync.
Use it
Open index.html in a browser, or host it anywhere static. The live site is https://sparkentry.sparkwaveitservice.com. On iPhone, use Share → Add to Home Screen and it opens full-screen like an app.
What it captures
Expenses with a category, note, and photo (camera or library)
Mileage with start and stop odometer readings and a live total
A copyable list of entries, with a checkbox to mark each one entered in your books
There is deliberately no export into a ledger — entries are added to your books by hand.
